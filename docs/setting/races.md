# Five peoples (working draft)

Status: **provisional**. Names and lore are a grillable starting set, not locked. Visuals still await the reference clip. Seeded worlds pick which peoples appear where; the first valley is usually one people plus a few outsiders.

Each people has a **dystopia trap** (what their order becomes under pressure) and a **utopia gift** (what they add to a felt harmony). None of this is a HUD bonus — it shows up in jobs, law, migration, and how people treat each other.

## 1. Hearthkin

Settled valley farmers and kiln-builders. They remember winters as personal debts.

- **Trap:** Close the gate. Outsiders starve outside a well-fed wall.
- **Gift:** Shared tables, seasonal surplus, patience with slow work.
- **Slice cue:** Hunger and shelter needs feel native here.

## 2. Vellum

Scribes, surveyors, oath-keepers. A promise written down outlives the speaker.

- **Trap:** Law as a cage. Procedure replaces mercy; paper outranks the person.
- **Gift:** Fair records, memory of harm, rules that can bind the powerful.
- **Slice cue:** Tax and inspecting a named person feel like their craft.

## 3. Cinderborn

Furnace towns, salvage yards, night shifts. They turn ruin into brick and tool.

- **Trap:** Extraction and class split. The works feed a few; smoke eats the rest.
- **Gift:** Industry that can house and heat many if surplus is not hoarded.
- **Slice cue:** Construction time and labor levies bite hardest in their stories.

## 4. Windset

Caravans, ferry clans, rumor routes. Home is a circuit, not a plot.

- **Trap:** No commons. Everyone is a guest; no one repairs the well.
- **Gift:** Trade, news, and the habit of leaving a place better than a raid.
- **Slice cue:** Migration and people walking out are their opinion meter.

## 5. Tideglass

Coastal choirs and tide-calendars. They prize stillness and shared ritual.

- **Trap:** Enforced peace. Dissent is a storm to be silenced.
- **Gift:** Voluntary quiet — people stay because they want to, not because they must.
- **Slice cue:** Hidden utopia cares about freedom, not just full bellies.
