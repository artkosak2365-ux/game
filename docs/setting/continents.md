# Seven continents (working draft)

Status: **provisional**. The world exists from seed zero, but a run **starts in one valley**. Other continents arrive as rumor, trade, refugees, and later diplomacy — not as a day-one grand map.

Seeds shuffle which people dominate which land. Names below are handles for the generator, not final lore.

## 1. Ashvale

Collapsed river kingdoms. Terraces, kiln-ruins, black soil. **Default first pocket.** Easy to farm, easy to starve if the wall goes up.

## 2. The Spine

A mountain chain treated as a continent by sailors who never cross it. Mines, passes, avalanches.

## 3. Greenwake

Long grassland and flood plains. Surplus here becomes tax and war later.

## 4. Saltreach

Broken coast and island chains. Unification without consent looks like a navy.

## 5. Umberwood

Old forest, canopy roads. Hard to census; easy to disappear. Opinion hides in the trees.

## 6. Glassdesert

Salt pans, glass dunes, night caravans. Scarcity makes every levy feel like theft.

## 7. Northveil

Ice shelves and dark-season towns. Heat is politics. A “utopia” that cannot winter is not a utopia.

## How a seed uses them

- Pick a **home continent** (weighted toward Ashvale for the vertical slice).
- Place 1–2 **neighbor signals** (a rumor, a caravan, a refugee).
- Do not simulate seven continents as individual agents.
