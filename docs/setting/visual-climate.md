# Visual climate (working draft)

Status: **awaiting reference film**. Camera and readability constraints from the bible are locked; palette and sprites wait for the clip.

## Locked

- Primary view: **2D from above**, readable at settlement scale.
- Optional **orthogonal / pitched** camera so terrain and buildings have depth (not a full 3D strategy cam).
- Simulation is independent of art. Placeholders are fine until the film lands.

## Placeholder until the clip

- Muted earth, ash, and a little gold (see `icon.svg`).
- No skeuomorphic “utopia bar”, no victory banner chrome.
- UI is clocks, jobs, names, and what people *do* — leaving, idling, building, paying.

## Open for later grilling

- Pixel vs painted vs vector tiles.
- How pitched is “ortho”?
- How much body language do agents need at zoom-out?
